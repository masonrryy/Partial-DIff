Physics Uncertainty Calculator

Enter a formula and the measured values with their uncertainties. The calculator evaluates the formula, finds its partial derivatives, and propagates independent uncertainties.

Formula

Examples: v = d / t, sqrt(x^2 + y^2), α * sin(θ). Use pi or π for pi, ^ for powers, and functions such as sin, cos, sqrt, and log. Trigonometric functions use radians.

Measured variables

Enter each symbol used in your formula, its measured value, and its absolute uncertainty (for example, 3.2 ± 0.1). Use the ×10^ button to enter scientific notation, such as 3.2 × 10^-4.

Result

Partial derivatives and uncertainty contributions

Assumption: input uncertainties are independent and are treated as standard uncertainties. Correlated measurements require covariance terms.
